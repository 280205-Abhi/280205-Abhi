## 📂 Featured Projects

### 🏨 [Hotel Booking Analysis](https://github.com/280205-Abhi/Hotel_Booking_Analysis)
End-to-end EDA on **119K+ booking records** to find what drives cancellations and where revenue leaks.

- Removed **31,994 duplicate rows (26.8%)** and engineered 8 new features
- Quantified **€11.5M in lost revenue (33% of potential)** from cancellations
- Logistic regression ranked cancellation drivers: **lead time** is the #1 driver, **special requests** the strongest protective factor
- Found the *non-refundable deposit paradox* and delivered 6 business recommendations (OTA policy, guest loyalty strategy)

`Python` `Pandas` `Seaborn` `Plotly` `Scikit-learn`

### 🧠 NeuroAI: Explainable Clinical Support for ASD Screening
Multimodal screening system combining **brain MRI, speech acoustics and the M-CHAT-R behavioural questionnaire**, built so that clinicians can see *why* a model said what it said.

- Clinical ensemble: Random Forest at **96.55% accuracy**, Gradient Boosting for severity
- MRI ensemble (ResNet18 + EfficientNet-B0): **84.21% accuracy, 0.9117 AUC**, with Grad-CAM++ heatmaps
- **17 models benchmarked**; SHAP explanations, a What-If simulator and auto-generated PDF reports
- FastAPI backend + React/Vite frontend

`Python` `PyTorch` `FastAPI` `React` `SHAP` `ReportLab`
